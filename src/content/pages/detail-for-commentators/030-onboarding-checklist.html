Commentary Checklist

PC or Laptop

Minimum: CPU Quad Core i5 1.4GHz, 8GB DDR3 memory, modern OS (Windows 10 or macOS 10.14+).

Internet

10 Mbps down / 10 Mbps up, ping < 100ms. Ethernet preferred. Keep a 4G backup connection on hand.

Google Chrome

The Spalk Commentary Studio works only in Google Chrome v93+. Update Chrome before every broadcast.

Microphone

Microphone recommendations:

  • Lip-Ribbon Microphones
  • USB streaming mics
  • XLR to USB converters or USB microphones acceptable

Headphones

Headphones or earphones are required. Never play output through external speakers. Wireless headphones are fine.

Environment

Quiet space. Close all windows, put a sign on the door, check for echo or hollow sound before going live.
